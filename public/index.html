Hadamard Initialization:

Applies $H^{\otimes 4}$ to place the initial state $|0000\rangle$ into a uniform superposition $\frac{1}{4} \sum_{k=0}^{15} |k\rangle$.

2
Single-Qubit Phase Rotations:

Encodes each scaled latent dimension $\theta_j \in [0, \pi]$ using single-qubit $R_Z(2\theta_j) = \exp(-i \theta_j Z_j)$.

3
Two-Qubit ZZ Entanglement:

Applies pairwise cross-feature rotations $R_{ZZ}\left(2(\pi - \theta_j)(\pi - \theta_k)\right)$, capturing complex non-linear autonomic correlations.

KERNEL OPERATOR

Quantum Fidelity Kernel

K(x, x') = |⟨Φ(x) | Φ(x')⟩|²

Computes the exact Hilbert-space transition probability (state overlap fidelity) between two parameterized quantum statevectors $|\Phi(x)\rangle$ and $|\Phi(x')\rangle$.

AUTOENCODER

Latent Feature Compression

z = \text{Encoder}(x) \in \mathbb{R}^4, \quad \theta_i = \pi \cdot \frac{z_i - z_{\min}}{z_{\max} - z_{\min}}

Compresses 10 collinear HRV features down to 4 orthogonal non-linear dimensions, mapped into $[0, \pi]$ bounds for optimal quantum phase rotations without $2\pi$ periodic aliasing.

CALIBRATION

Platt Sigmoid Scaling

P(y=1 \mid f) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(A \cdot f(x) + B)}

Transforms raw SVM geometric margin decision scores $f(x)$ into well-calibrated posterior probabilities with an Expected Calibration Error of only 0.0022.

OPTIMIZATION

J(\tau) = \text{Sensitivity}(\tau) + \text{Specificity}(\tau) - 1
